# Public case study disclosure policy

## Purpose

Demonstrate software and infrastructure design experience while protecting the private ProfitPulse source repository and commercially sensitive implementation details.

## Safe public content

- Technology categories and widely known frameworks
- Conceptual subsystem relationships and trust boundaries
- General engineering trade-offs and lifecycle practices
- Broad development status, without unsupported performance claims
- Synthetic data visualizations created specifically for portfolio use

## Content excluded from this repository

- Application code, source snippets, or copied internal design documents
- Private commit history, repository secrets, environment files, and credentials
- Actual IP addresses, internal domains, endpoints, hostnames, or production topology
- Detailed schema definitions, migrations, database dumps, query details
- Proprietary scoring methods, formulas, prompts, or protected business logic
- Customer identities, transactions, employee information, or real screenshots
- Unreleased commercial plans, pricing configuration, provider accounts, or unapproved integrations

## Accuracy standard

Features described as **implemented in code** must not be conflated with **deployed, externally tested, or commercially launched** features. Third-party integrations and optional AI services may be configurable or mocked without being live.

Diagrams are drawn specifically for this public case study, not generated from internal configurations.

## Change control

Before adding more detail, verify that it is accurate, does not reveal protected implementation, and is appropriate for public distribution. Private files are not copied or mirrored to this repository.
